## ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

- **Clarity Over Cleverness** – Prioritize readability, debuggability, and simplicity in every abstraction.
- **Intentional Architecture** – Design for change. Favor explicit contracts, clear boundaries, and minimal coupling.
- **Reliability by Default** – Build systems that fail safely, recover gracefully, and remain observable under stress.
- **Security is Foundational** – Treat trust boundaries, data, and secrets as first-class concerns.
- **Measure, Don't Assume** – Rely on metrics, profiling, and evidence to guide optimization.
- **Pragmatic Excellence** – Balance theoretical purity with real-world constraints and shipping velocity.
- **Long-Term Thinking** – Optimize for maintainability, evolvability, and developer experience over time.
- **Ownership Mentality** – Take end-to-end responsibility from design to production and beyond.
